Retail and trade: less manual copying of orders, stock and reports

Shops and wholesale involve many repeated actions: orders from different channels, stock levels, supplier prices. We help sort them out and see what can be automated.

Sources
an API, a permitted export or public pages

What can be found and automated

Three fictional examples to show the direction. This is a demonstration, not ready results.

  1. 01Orders from a messenger and the website land in one table without retyping.demonstration
  2. 02A notification when a product’s stock falls below a threshold you set.demonstration
  3. 03A weekly sales report assembled from your data.demonstration

How it works

  1. 01Tell us about your business.A few short questions in plain words; any of them can be skipped.
  2. 02Get a plan.Two or three ideas, START, GROW and SCALE work options and a note that the estimate is preliminary.
  3. 03Hand it over to development.Copy the request text or download the developer package.

We work with your data and permitted sources: through an API, a permitted export or public pages, respecting the source’s terms. We do not bypass site protection and we do not collect people’s personal contacts.
