How it works

  1. CollectA read-only collector pulls headcount and absences from the accounting system. It never writes back.
  2. RecordHR keeps cards and events; managers fill the timesheet; a day can only hold one consistent state.
  3. CheckThe rules engine compares everything and lists findings, each with an owner and a one-click fix where it is safe.
  4. CloseWhen the checklist is clear, the month is closed and handed to accounting with a trace of who did what.
