Case / Subscription billing / Payment recovery

Failed card payments should not end a subscription

A subscription business was losing paying customers to expired cards and declined charges. Most of them never meant to leave, they simply never noticed the failed payment.

We built a system that listens to payment events, opens a request, picks a tone by the reason of the failure, writes a short human email and gives the customer a one-click page to fix the card. Recovery Desk below is a fictional reworking of this idea, and everything in it works.

Integrations, queue and notifications

Payment provider

Events arrive as signed automatic notifications from the payment system. A bad signature is rejected, a repeated event is recognised and ignored, and a second failure for the same customer joins the open case.

Email and AI

Letters go through a queue with retries and delivery tracking. If the email provider or the AI model is down, the system keeps working: messages wait, and drafts fall back to fixed templates.

Notifications that respect people

A send hour, a weekly cap per customer, opt-outs and a spam-complaint stop. Serious cases such as suspected fraud skip the campaign and go to a person.

How it works

  1. 1

    Listen

    The payment provider sends an event. The system checks the signature, ignores repeats and opens a request.

  2. 2

    Decide

    Rules read the reason of the failure and choose the tone, the template and the timing. Serious cases go to a person.

  3. 3

    Write and send

    An AI model drafts a short human letter inside guardrails; a queue sends it with retries and tracks delivery.

  4. 4

    Recover and learn

    The customer updates the card in one click, the case closes and analytics show which step worked.
