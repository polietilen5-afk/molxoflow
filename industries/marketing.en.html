Marketing: watch the market, competitors and mentions

Agencies and marketers need to see what changes around them: competitor promotions, prices, brand mentions. We help watch this by your criteria and get notified.

What can be found and automated

Three fictional examples to show the direction. This is a demonstration, not ready results.

  • demonstration A list of new promotions and price changes at chosen competitors with a link to the source.
  • demonstration A notification about a brand or topic mention on public platforms.
  • demonstration A summary of key metrics from your accounts in one table.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your business. A few short questions in plain words; any of them can be skipped.
  2. Get a plan. Two or three ideas, START, GROW and SCALE work options and a note that the estimate is preliminary.
  3. Hand it over to development. Copy the request text or download the developer package; the site sends nothing by itself.

We work with your data and permitted sources: through an API, a permitted export or public pages, respecting the source’s terms. We do not bypass site protection and we do not collect people’s personal contacts.
