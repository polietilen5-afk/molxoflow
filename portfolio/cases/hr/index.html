Case: staff records, timesheets and checks

Staff records, timesheets and payroll checks stop living in five different files

Crew Ledger is an internal application for a mid-size operating company: one staff register, HR events with approvals, one timesheet, and a checklist that catches gaps before anyone calculates pay.

  • Web app
  • Python
  • PostgreSQL
  • Role-based access
  • Read-only accounting sync

Try the demo Discuss a similar project

The problem, the solution, the result

1

The problem

Staff data, attendance, absences and output memos were kept in separate spreadsheets. Mistakes surfaced only at payroll time, and everyone saw more than they should.

2

What we built

A full application: staff register and cards, HR events with approval chains, a timesheet with approval cycles and versions, piecework and memos, automatic rules, an accounting sync that only reads, reports, audit and backups. Every role gets its own view of the data.

3

The result

All the data in one place. Problems are found while the month is still open, every change is traceable, and sensitive fields stay with the people who need them.

Integrations and notifications

  • Accounting system, read-onlyA collector pulls staff and absences on request or on schedule and can never write back.
  • Spreadsheet import and exportTimesheet and reports leave as spreadsheet files; an optional validated import is switched on in settings.
  • In-app remindersProbation ends, unclosed timesheets and open discrepancies appear as tasks for the right role.
  • Audit and backupsChanges are journaled without personal data or amounts; backups are verified by test restores.

How it works

  1. CollectA read-only collector pulls headcount and absences from the accounting system. It never writes back.
  2. RecordHR keeps cards and events; managers fill the timesheet; a day can only hold one consistent state.
  3. CheckThe automatic rules compare everything and lists findings, each with an owner and a one-click fix where it is safe.
  4. CloseWhen the checklist is clear, the month is approved and handed to accounting with a trace of who did what.
