Nobody reads a hundred-page tender at midnight any more

Bid Radar collects tenders from mailboxes and portals, reads every document, pulls out requirements and deadlines and checks them against the company profile and its certificates.

The problem

Tenders arrive by email and on portals as long PDF, DOCX and XLSX files. Reading them takes days, certificates expire unnoticed, deadlines slip and good tenders get missed.

2

What we built

A workspace for several companies at once: intake with rules and de-duplication, scan reading and AI extraction with confidence, qualification against the company profile, cost sheets, a guided bid workflow, calendar and analytics.

3

The result

From a pile of attachments to a clear decision. Every value links back to the exact fragment of the source, people review only what the AI is unsure about, and every action is logged.

How it works

  1. 1

    Collect

    Mailboxes and portals feed one queue. Rules filter noise, duplicates and updated notices are recognised.

  2. 2

    Read

    PDF, DOCX and XLSX go through scan reading and layout parsing; every fact keeps a link to its place in the source.

  3. 3

    Decide

    Extracted requirements are compared with the company profile and certificates; the team sees a bid / skip call with reasons.

  4. 4

    Prepare

    A durable workflow assembles the package, asks for approval, tracks every step and shows where a bid is stuck.

Stack and approach

  • Modular backend
  • Durable workflows
  • Document parsing and scan reading
  • AI connection with human review
  • Vector search
  • Tracing and metrics
  • Tenant isolation
