Real estate: find undervalued properties and stop losing leads

Agencies and private investors have to go through dozens of listings and spreadsheets by hand. We help gather suitable properties in one place and answer clients faster.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your business. A few short questions in plain words; any of them can be skipped.
  2. Get a plan. Two or three ideas, START, GROW and SCALE work options and a note that the estimate is preliminary.
  3. Hand it over to development. Copy the request text or download the developer package; the site sends nothing by itself.

We work only with permitted sources: through an API, a permitted export or public pages, respecting the source’s terms. We do not offer collecting people’s personal contacts.
