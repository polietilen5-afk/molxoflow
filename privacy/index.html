Privacy

What the site stores and where it connects

Short and factual: this describes the current state of the site. We do not collect email, phone or other personal data, and there is no request form on the site.

  • What is stored in your browser

    Only the interface language you picked (the molxo-variant-lang key in localStorage). It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. The site uses no cookies and no analytics.

  • Outside requests

    The fonts are stored on the site itself, so the pages do not contact Google Fonts or any other outside service while loading. The only outside link is the Telegram button, and it works only when you click it. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

  • Contact

    The only way to reach us on the site is Telegram @Molxo1. The buttons just open Telegram through a link. A message is sent only if you write and send it there yourself.

  • Portfolio demos

    Data in the demos is fictional. Fields and forms inside a demo work in the browser only and send nothing anywhere.

  • Questions

    If something is unclear, write to us on Telegram. We do not list any other contacts for this.
