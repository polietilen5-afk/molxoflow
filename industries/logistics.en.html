Industry: Production and logistics

Production and logistics: statuses and reports without manual copying

When orders, deliveries and stock live in different spreadsheets and chats, a lot of time goes into reconciling them. We help bring them together and see what can be automated.

Sources
an API or a permitted export

What can be found and automated

Three fictional examples to show the direction. This is a demonstration, not ready results.

  1. 01Order and delivery statuses from different spreadsheets are collected into one summary.demonstration
  2. 02A notification to the person in charge if an order deadline moves.demonstration
  3. 03A daily report on stock of materials or goods from your data.demonstration

How it works

  1. 01Tell us about your business.A few short questions in plain words; any of them can be skipped.
  2. 02Get a plan.Two or three ideas, START, GROW and SCALE work options and a note that the estimate is preliminary.
  3. 03Hand it over to development.Copy the request text or download the developer package.

We work with your data and permitted sources: through an API or a permitted export. We do not bypass site protection and we do not collect people’s personal contacts.
