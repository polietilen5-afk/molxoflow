Know what is wrong with the car before you reach the garage

A car owner hears a noise or sees a warning light and has no idea whether it is a minor fix or a safety risk, or what a fair price looks like.

We built a mobile app: pick the symptoms, answer a few questions from an AI diagnostician and get ranked causes, urgency, a check list and a price range, then book a service in the same app.

1

Problem

Help drivers understand a car problem in plain words: how serious it is, what is probably broken, what it may cost, and what to do next, without a call to a garage first.

2

What we built

A cross-platform mobile app with a symptom wizard, a scripted-and-AI dialogue, ranked causes with confidence, a price calculator by car class, region and service level, an error code catalog, booking, history and reminders.

3

Result

One place for a car’s story: every diagnosis, estimate, booking and service reminder is linked to the car, and the app still answers when the AI model is unavailable.

        Integrations and behind the screen

        AI model with a local fallback

        Diagnosis goes to a language model through a single gateway with a timeout and a retry. If it is slow or down, a built-in knowledge base answers, so the app never ends on an error screen.

        Price calculator

        Prices are calculated from labour hours, a car class factor, a regional factor and a service level, with a range instead of a false exact number. Currency and rates are configuration.

        Data on the device

        Cars, history and settings are stored on the phone, with secure storage for secrets. Calendar and push notifications connect to the reminders and bookings.

        How it works

        1. 1

          Describe

          Choose the car and the system, tick the symptoms and add a note.

        2. 2

          Clarify

          The AI diagnostician asks two or three targeted questions to separate close causes.

        3. 3

          Understand

          Ranked causes, urgency, a check list and a price range by region and service level.

        4. 4

          Act

          Book a service, track the status, get reminders and keep the car’s full history.
