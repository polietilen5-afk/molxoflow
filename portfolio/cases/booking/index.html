Case: online booking for clinics and service centers

Bookings stop living in phone calls, chats and a paper diary

Booking Desk is a subscription service for clinics and auto services: a public booking page, a schedule that cannot be double-booked, a client base, an AI consultant, a Telegram bot, reminders and analytics, all on one set of data.

  • SaaS
  • React / TypeScript
  • Python / FastAPI
  • AI consultant
  • Telegram bot

Try the demo Discuss a similar project

1

The problem

Requests arrived by phone, in messengers and by word of mouth. Slots were double-booked, clients forgot visits, and the same questions about prices and hours were answered by hand again and again.

2

What we built

One platform for several businesses: a branded public booking page, a schedule with working hours, breaks and time off, a client base, services and prices, an AI consultant that answers only from the knowledge base, a Telegram bot for clients and for the team, reminders, analytics, roles and an audit log.

3

The result

One source of truth for every booking channel. A taken slot disappears everywhere at once, the team confirms requests from a phone, clients get reminders on time, and routine questions are answered without anyone picking up the phone.

How it works

  1. BookA client picks a service, a specialist and a free time on the booking page, in the bot or in the chat with the assistant.
  2. CheckThe booking rules build slots from working hours, breaks, time off and existing bookings, so a taken slot cannot be booked twice.
  3. ConfirmThe team gets an instant message, confirms or declines in one tap, or reschedules from the calendar. Every step is logged.
  4. Remind and learnReminders go out on schedule, no-shows are counted, and analytics shows channels, load and busy hours.
