Services: requests and bookings without manual routine

In a service business, requests come from many places and clients need answers and reminders. We help bring this into one place and see what can be automated.

What can be found and automated

Three fictional examples to show the direction. This is a demonstration, not ready results.

  • demonstration Requests from a messenger, email and a website form are collected into one list.
  • demonstration An automatic reply to frequent client questions, passing complex ones to a staff member.
  • demonstration A reminder to the client about a booking the day before the visit.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your business. A few short questions in plain words; any of them can be skipped.
  2. Get a plan. Two or three opportunities, START, GROW and SCALE work options and a note that the estimate is preliminary.
  3. Hand it over to development. Copy the request text or download the developer package; the site sends nothing by itself.

We work with your data and permitted sources. We do not bypass site protection and we do not collect people’s personal contacts without their consent.
